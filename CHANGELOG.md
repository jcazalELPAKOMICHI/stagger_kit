## 0.1.0

- Initial release.
- `List<Widget>.stagger()` and `Widget.animateIn()` extensions.
- Effects: `FadeEffect`, `MoveEffect`, `SlideEffect`, `ScaleEffect`,
  `BlurEffect`, `CustomEffect`, plus `StaggerPresets`.
- `StaggerScope` and `StaggerIndexed` for lazy lists and grids, with
  diagonal cascades for grids.
- `StaggerController` to play, replay, reverse and reset a group.
- `StaggerOrder`: forward, reverse and center-out.
- Respects the platform's reduced-motion setting.
